    COMMON QUESTIONS

    Good questions,
    straight answers.

    Can't see yours? Ask us directly ↗

    Will our data stay in Australia?

    Yes, by default. We build in the AWS Sydney (ap-southeast-2) and Melbourne (ap-southeast-4) regions and use account-level guardrails to stop resources being created elsewhere. For generative AI, we confirm which models are available in Australian regions and how requests are processed before anything goes live, so you can make an informed decision.

    Do you work with small and medium businesses?

    Absolutely. Many of our engagements start with a fixed-scope package so you know the cost and outcome upfront. We explain things in plain English and build only what you need.

    Can you help us with the Essential Eight?

    Yes. We help apply the Essential Eight mitigation strategies to your cloud and identity setup, automate the controls where possible and prepare evidence. We don't issue certifications; we get you ready for your assessor or auditor.

    How do you approach responsible and safe AI?

    We follow Australia's AI Ethics Principles and the guardrails in the Voluntary AI Safety Standard. In practice, that means a clear purpose, testing and evaluation before launch, privacy impact thinking, human oversight for important decisions and records of how the system works.

    Do you only work with AWS?

    We're AWS-first because it's where we go deepest, but our solutions connect to the tools you already use, such as Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Xero, Salesforce, MYOB and custom APIs.

    Is there any funding available?

    Possibly. Some AWS migration and modernisation projects can qualify for AWS funding programs or credits, subject to AWS eligibility and approval, and we'll help you check. Innovative AI development may be eligible for the R&D Tax Incentive; we provide the technical records and your tax adviser confirms eligibility.

    How do you price your work?

    Packages are fixed-fee. Larger projects are priced by milestone or as embedded capacity. All quotes are in Australian dollars with GST clearly shown, and we agree the scope in writing before work begins.

    Where are you based?

    We're an Australian company, Maitighar AI Pty Ltd, working remotely with clients nationally on AEST/AEDT hours and on-site by arrangement. We also support clients in New Zealand.
